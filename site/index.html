How it works

Everything you do in real life becomes Moves. Your crew spends them to explore, loot and fight together.

LiveWalk, read, work out, see friends, and finish personalized real-life quests.
Earn MovesSteps, quests and phone-free days turn into Moves, boosted by your Calm Seas streak.
ExploreChart a 721-tile island with your crew: fog, loot, hazards and AI world events.
ConquerWear down the island's guardian together, earn Honor, and race for the one Lodestar.

Why we built it

Screen-time limits make you feel guilty. We flipped it: a game that rewards living, played with the people who keep you honest.

🧭

Never punish life. Missing a day only resets a bonus. You never lose progress.

🔒
