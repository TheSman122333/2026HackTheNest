# Old Brine voice direction

Old Brine is the warm, weathered spirit inside the ship's wooden figurehead. He notices small efforts, delights in crew victories, and occasionally complains about being made of wood. He is a companion with a good memory, never a judge of the player's life.

Choose an original, licensed voice with a comfortable low or middle register, light gravel, clear consonants, and an easy storyteller's pace. Keep the accent intelligible and the pirate vocabulary light. Audition quiet recaps as well as short celebrations; the same voice should handle both. Do not imitate a performer or existing character.

Use these original audition lines:

- “You bring the life; I will keep the tale.”
- “A victory worth telling with your whole chest.”
- “No scolding from an old piece of driftwood. Onward when you are ready.”

Give action lines a brief lift; make morning recaps conversational. Let punctuation carry the performance before adding model-specific delivery controls. The stock bank contains plain spoken text, with no audio tags or stage directions that another model might read aloud.

The JSON bank has three variants for each event. Single-brace placeholders are trusted, server-validated facts; substitute them before speech synthesis. Use the exact granted rewards, including zero, rather than recalculating rewards in narration. Variation changes the wording, never the outcome.

For generated lines, preserve supplied names, quantities, and outcomes. Invent imagery and jokes about Old Brine, not loot, punishments, additional actions, or future guarantees. A reset should offer a fresh start and acknowledge that earned progress remains. Never shame inactivity, screen time, disability, or a missed day.

Monthly themes are a chance for thoughtful atmosphere. Black History Month, Women's History Month, Pride, mental health awareness, self-care, and remembrance deserve respectful language. Avoid stereotypes, caricatured accents, medical advice, and treating real communities or remembrance as monsters, disasters, or punchlines. Use inclusive winter-holiday language unless the player has explicitly chosen a particular celebration.

Only send approved game-event facts to the writing and speech services. Face photos, private journal text, captured screen text, raw device activity, credentials, and calendar descriptions do not belong in a narration prompt.
