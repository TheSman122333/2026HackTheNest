The Last Voyage

Full version

Android

Counts your steps for you and tracks how much you scroll, which unlocks the Calm Seas streak.

  1. Tap Download APK.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  3. Open The Last Voyage and sign in.
Download APK You can also just play in the browser.

iPhone & iPad

No App Store needed. The game installs from Safari as a home screen app.

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Play on iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open it from your home screen. It runs full screen and stays signed in.
Play on iPhone On iPhone you type in your steps from the Health app, and you get 2 extra quests a day to make up for it.

Computer

Works in any browser. Handy for checking on your crew or fighting the boss on a bigger screen.

  1. Click Play in browser.
  2. Sign in with your email, or use Quick join.

How it works

Things you do in real life earn you Moves. You and your crew spend them on the island.

Go do stuffWalk, work out, read, see friends, or finish one of your daily quests.
Earn MovesSteps, quests and days spent off short-form video all add up. Keep a streak going for a bonus.
ExploreMove around a big island with your crew, pick up loot and avoid hazards.
Beat the bossTake turns hitting the island's boss. Every crewmate's damage counts.

Why we made it

Screen-time apps mostly make you feel bad about scrolling. We wanted something that gives you a reason to put the phone down, with friends keeping each other going.

⚓

You never lose progress. Missing a day only resets your streak bonus.

🔒
